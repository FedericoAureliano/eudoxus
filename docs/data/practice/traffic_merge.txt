Model a traffic merging control system for a highway on-ramp with traffic lights that control when cars can enter the highway.
The system monitors traffic density on both the highway and on-ramp, and adjusts the timing of the merge-control traffic light accordingly. During peak hours, the system implements a more restrictive merging pattern.
Define transitions based on traffic density measurements, time of day, and special conditions (accidents, construction, emergency vehicles).
Use variable names: highway_density, ramp_density, light_state, cars_waiting, peak_hour, emergency_vehicle, current_cycle_time.
