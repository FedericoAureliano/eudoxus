Model an airport runway management system that controls takeoffs and landings on a single runway. The runway can be in states: available, reserved_for_takeoff, reserved_for_landing, in_use_takeoff, in_use_landing, and emergency.
The system should prioritize landings over takeoffs under normal conditions, but emergency landings have highest priority.
Define transitions based on aircraft requests, runway clearance, weather conditions, and emergency situations.
Use variable names: runway_state, takeoff_queue, landing_queue, emergency_requests, weather_condition, runway_clear_time.
