Model a coffee shop order system. Customers can order either coffee or tea. The system tracks orders, serves drinks, and handles payments.
The system has three locations: idle, order_placed, and payment. Variables track the number of coffee orders (coffee_orders) and tea orders (tea_orders).
Define transitions for:

Taking an order (coffee or tea)
Serving drinks when orders exist
Handling payment
Canceling orders

Use variables: coffee_orders, tea_orders, order_coffee, order_tea, serve_coffee, serve_tea, pay, cancel
