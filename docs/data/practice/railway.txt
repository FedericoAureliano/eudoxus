Model a railway junction controller for a Y-shaped track junction where trains can arrive from three directions: north, east, and west. Only one train can pass through the junction at a time.
The controller must ensure trains are properly sequenced through the junction, with priority given to emergency vehicles and following a fair scheduling policy for regular trains.
Define states for the junction (empty, occupied_from_north, occupied_from_east, occupied_from_west) and transitions based on train arrivals, junction clearance, and priority rules.
Use variable names: junction_state, north_train_waiting, east_train_waiting, west_train_waiting, emergency_train, wait_time_north, wait_time_east, wait_time_west.
