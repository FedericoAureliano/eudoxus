Model a water tank control system for a tank with input and output valves. The system monitors water level and controls the valves to maintain water between minimum and maximum levels.
The system has states for normal operation, filling, draining, overflow risk, and empty risk. It should respond to both automatic level sensors and manual override commands.
Define transitions between states based on water level readings, valve positions, and manual control inputs.
Use variable names: water_level, input_valve, output_valve, system_state, manual_override, alarm_state.
