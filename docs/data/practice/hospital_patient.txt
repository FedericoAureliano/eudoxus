Model a hospital patient monitoring system that tracks vital signs and alerts medical staff when readings are outside normal ranges.
The system has states for normal monitoring, warning (one vital sign outside range), critical (multiple vital signs outside range), and alarm silenced (temporary suspension of audible alerts).
Define transitions between states based on vital sign readings, staff acknowledgment of alerts, and timer expirations for silenced alarms.
Use variable names: heart_rate, blood_pressure, oxygen, temperature, system_state, alarm_silence_timer, staff_acknowledged.
