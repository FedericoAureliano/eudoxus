Model a smart home lighting system for a house with three rooms (living room, kitchen, bedroom). Each room has a light that can be turned on/off manually or by motion sensors.
The system should also have an "away mode" where all lights automatically turn off after 10 minutes of no motion, and a "night mode" where lights turn on at reduced brightness.
Define transitions between different system states and how the controller manages the three lights based on motion detection, manual controls, and mode settings.
Use variable names: living_light, kitchen_light, bedroom_light, motion_living, motion_kitchen, motion_bedroom, away_mode, night_mode, timer.
